# 27.0.43 Resume protected stock plans without a recovery/replan loop

## Evidence

The new 27.0.42 diagnostic repeatedly confirms `FOREIGN_PURCHASE_ISOLATED` for the historical Set 9 / Challenge 24 record. The current Set 60 / Challenge 100 retains 11,680 / 50,000 confirmed points. Its original remainder then fails material validation with `POLICY_CHANGED`. Two newly generated all-Club plans (22 cards, no purchases, 38,330 additional points) fail preflight with `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` instead.

The export does not name the changed policy field. Production-composition regressions independently reproduce both errors: adding an unrelated manual lock or roster member invalidates an old frozen policy, and a journal containing confirmed plus waiting batches blocks a fresh remainder plan. This is not evidence of another SBC currently running, lost points or a newly dispatched contribution.

## Changes

- Treat exact lock/active-squad membership as current protection evidence, not immutable selection settings. Current membership is read and enforced at validation and immediately before dispatch. Selected locked or active-squad cards still stop; missing protection evidence still stops. Real selection-policy changes are not silently accepted.
- A new explicitly approved stock plan may replace only a fully observed, partially confirmed Club/Storage journal at exactly its durable confirmed score, for the same account and target. All confirmed batches need accepted receipts. No pending/unknown write, abnormal progress, purchase receipt/scope, market route, external move or already-consumed physical item is eligible.
- Preserve the complete original journal under an account-scoped revision history key and verify its readback before replacing the active journal. Archive/checkpoint failures keep the evidence and send no contribution. Retry reuses the exact archive without counting or contributing twice.
- The normal independent EA progress, selected-material, identity, protection and dispatch checks still run on the newly approved plan. A stale score or newly protected card cannot gain approval through replanning. No old approval or budget is extended.
- Keep 27.0.42 historical purchase isolation, pending-write protection, native Puzzle reservation-release and purchase-handoff fixes. Unknown historical purchases are not erased or guessed successful. Gallery/Puzzle selection, FSU source and shared native buy/move methods are unchanged.

The new replacement path is stock-only. Purchased/external-card journals retain their existing dedicated recovery requirements. Generating a plan alone does not archive, replace or contribute anything; a new trusted execution click is required.
