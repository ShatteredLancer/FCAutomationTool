## Evidence

The new 27.0.43 diagnostic confirms one successful 1,250-point SBC contribution using four owned cards: EA accepted the response, the receipt was verified, independent readback confirmed completion, and the run returned `completed`. This is evidence of that operation, not completion of the older 50,000-point task or a reward claim.

The following attempts report `INITIATION_UNVERIFIED`, recovery `CONTEXT_CHANGED`, and preflight `PURCHASE_CONTRIBUTION_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`. The historical purchase marker remains local evidence, not proof of another live task. A production-composition replay reproduces the same-target handoff failure with a valid old marker and a disjoint, confirmed stock cycle. The export does not expose their physical IDs/fingerprints; the replay is not presented as an exact reconstruction of those private records.

## Changes

- Target IDs alone no longer bind an outer purchase marker to the contribution journal being replaced. Validate its complete frozen batch plan, context, target, starting score and material first. Only the exact matching batch can settle its outer bookkeeping.
- A different frozen batch whose full historical reserved scope is disjoint from the confirmed stock journal remains unchanged. It cannot block replacement solely because a repeatable SBC reuses the same Set/Challenge IDs. Overlap, malformed scope or missing proof still blocks; no old purchase is erased or presumed successful.
- Apply historical reservation checks even when IDs match. The exact active purchase batch retains its existing receipt-scoped path only through its owning procurement driver; an independent stock click cannot inherit its write authority. Unrelated Club/Storage plans must prove stock-only isolation during validation and again inside native dispatch. Buy/move/receipt routes do not inherit this exception.
- Same-target independent stock recovery no longer enters an old buyer adapter with a stale score/rule snapshot. A genuine matching purchase batch still uses its dedicated read-only recovery. Changed target scores are explained as scoped historical recovery, not treated as permission to rewrite the old plan.
- A fully confirmed contribution journal with an accepted completion receipt may be displayed as **上一轮已确认** when the current page has reset. Its old points are not current-cycle progress, the contribution button stays disabled, and no EA mutation is sent by this display/recovery.
- Keep the existing native `NOT_STARTED` initiation guard. Re-enter/start the current cycle through EA's native interface before generating its new plan. This update adds no initiation, buying, moving, submission retry or reward-claim capability.
- A fixed `purchase-history-overlap` diagnostic distinguishes a real shared-entity conflict without exporting entity IDs, accounts, receipts or raw responses. All .42–.43 protections, unknown-write handling, reservation-release and stock-replan fixes remain.
