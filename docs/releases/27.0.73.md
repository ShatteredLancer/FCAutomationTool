# 27.0.73 — Preserve single-club planning across return-to-window refreshes

- Routine visibility/timer catalogue checks wait while a single-club plan or grade-cost overview is running. Repeated checks coalesce into one refresh after completion; explicit observations still validate changed rules/materials and frozen quote expiry.
- The running plan surface is keyed by this collection's actual rules/materials, not the whole catalogue revision. An unrelated collection update followed by a filter redraw no longer cancels the calculation.
- Cancellation, expiry and error explanations survive subsequent same-page catalogue/detail redraws. The requested grade also survives filtering and asynchronous score repaint, rather than reverting from A to D. This small presentation state is bounded to 20 collections in account-scoped memory; retry/change of grade clears old errors, account/source changes clear all. It is neither quotes nor purchase approvals. No new persistent snapshot or transaction history is added.

Two production-UI regressions failed against the original .72 source: a quote-expiry notice became blank after `visibilitychange`, and an unrelated catalogue revision followed by filtering cancelled the run. The routine-refresh deferral assertion also failed before this patch. The expanded offline Chrome matrix covers quote-time/compute-time return, repeated timer checks, plan/overview expiry followed by return, cancellation/error retry, real rule changes and account isolation. Network access is blocked in those tests.

This does not change solver order/limits, pricing sources/TTL, budgets, single-card caps, FSU/locks, purchases/listings, journals or recovery. It does not claim to solve browser background throttling or the planner's remaining CPU cost. A controlled Brentford replay with older card facts and 24 later retained public quotes measured ~0.2s at 100 evaluations versus ~11.6s at 30,000 (5,400 vs 5,200 estimated coins); it is not an exact replay of the cancelled live call or a live-speed guarantee. See the [investigation and acceptance record](../FC27_GALLERY_PLAN_REFRESH_ZH.md).

No live account transactions, claims, submissions or script installation were performed during validation. Live return-to-window acceptance remains distinct from the synthetic browser regressions.
