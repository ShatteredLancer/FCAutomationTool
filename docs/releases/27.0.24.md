# 27.0.24 — Points Solver purchasing and English UI

## Changes

- Keep **Lowest total material value** as the default. More batches and longer
  waits are accepted; execution does not pay a premium just to finish faster.
- Review the selected stock cards, approved market substitution pool and total
  purchase budget, then click **Buy & contribute** once. The same action accepts
  the displayed limited quote coverage; no extra coverage checkbox is needed.
- Replan within the frozen market pool and total budget after availability
  blockers. Only the displayed selected stock IDs can be consumed. A held
  Club version does not authorize consuming an existing protected copy.
- Retry ordinary empty supply twice with 15-second waits. Normal scarcity can
  be retried with the execution button, without mandatory receipt recovery.
- Show separate capacity, held-version, budget, quote and search-failure reasons.
  Actual unknown purchase/move/contribution outcomes still require reconciliation.
- Treat price caps as upper bounds, not required spend. Search with the lesser
  of the frozen card cap and remaining budget rather than skipping potentially
  affordable offers. Known bid rejections are not misreported as empty supply.
- Archive settled runs before replacing them under a new explicit approval.
  Retain unconsumed purchases and pending writes; never clear them to replan.
- Verify pool holdings through the existing owned native Club read transport
  rather than partial/stale cache membership. Preserve exact-version checks.
- Show execution progress and candidate scope in English. Points Solver UI,
  settings, protection explanations and warnings are now English throughout.
  Native player/SBC names are retained. This is not a full translation of all
  existing FCAT modules.

## Boundaries

FSU 26.09.10 and the shared Gallery/Puzzle native buyer are unchanged. The
existing economic solver, price-source policy, locks/active-squad protection,
request fingerprints, transaction mutex and intent-before-write checkpoints
are retained. Old journals keep their original frozen route-only permissions.
Approval still lasts up to ten minutes per trusted execution click; confirmed
progress and receipts are retained when it expires or the user stops.

Lowest cost is based on the verified quoted pool and bounded planning search,
not a guarantee of global optimality or current auction supply. Each selected
version's live quote is refreshed without increasing its approved cap.
