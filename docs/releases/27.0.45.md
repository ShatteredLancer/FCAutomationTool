## Changes

- After read-only recovery, a separate **退出并归档旧任务** action can retire an abandoned *other-target* local run. No solve, recovery, timeout, script update or ordinary execute click silently discards it. The action performs local storage writes only, never EA purchases, movements, contributions or claims.
- Under the shared account lock, recheck the exact reviewed outer and inner snapshots. Save the complete original purchase journal to history, then persist and read back a bounded retirement index. Do not change its receipts, spent coins, consumed IDs, contribution states or confirmed score; retirement is not settlement.
- Unresolved buy/move requests remain in recovery. An unresolved inner contribution can be retired only when its full frozen plan matches the historical outer marker. Preserve that entire journal, including pending/unknown evidence. An unrelated current contribution cannot be archived through this action. A partially completed old-target journal can retain its confirmed batches and cancel local continuation without inventing results.
- Only a fully matching archived inner journal is omitted from the *active* contribution view. Its original persisted revision stays intact until a separately approved new execution replaces it with the next revision. Stale journal writers are rejected. No global storage clear, new journal state or automatic historical request retry is introduced.
- Quarantine exact historical reserved entities, including waiting/pending/unknown inner-batch material. Exclude them while planning, check new purchase checkpoints and pre-request checks, and recheck contribution material inside native dispatch. Another physical copy of the same version is not quarantined merely by definition ID. The original SBC remains blocked while its archived contribution outcome is unknown.
- Verify complete selected-inventory ledger coverage before archival; missing entries cannot silently release cards. Reject incoming archived fingerprints before starting a replacement journal. Before new procurement can spend, check that the current inner journal is absent/archived, unsent, fully terminal, or the exact active purchase's own batch; unresolved or non-replaceable partial work stops before any new buy/move.
- The new other-target market run requires a fresh plan and explicit independent budget. Never inherit the historical remaining budget or resume its buying authority. All FSU, lock, active-squad, rating, price-cap, account and transaction guards remain. Gallery, ordinary Puzzle, FSU and native contracts are unchanged.
- If execution hits the foreign purchase blocker, inspect its local history branch immediately and offer the same explicit archive action. Do not display the historical target's 430 points or 1,650 coins as current-plan progress/spend. Archival clears the preview and cannot execute the old or new plan automatically.
- Corrupt/conflicting archives, changed account/page/records, failed persistence and a full 50-record index stop without deleting evidence. The button is not offered for missing/malformed contribution scope.
