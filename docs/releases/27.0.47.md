## Changes

- After confirmed readback/finalization, wait until a 3-second cooldown has passed before the next batch's fresh validation. Preserve the last confirmed time in the current root and use the durable confirmed journal timestamp after provider recreation. Do not delay the final writeback, reuse a stale validation lease or skip fresh material/score checks after waiting.
- Only the hash-reviewed progress GET adds the observed 426/512 statuses to bounded recovery: wait 3 seconds, then 6 seconds, at most three GET attempts in the original shared 15-second deadline. Existing transient network statuses retain their 1.5/3-second backoff. This is not a global reclassification of EA errors and never retries a contribution POST.
- Authentication, explicit rate limits, conflicting/unknown native codes, malformed metadata, missing identity, changed controller/account/method and exhausted deadlines still stop. Release observers/timers, ignore late replies, and keep the durable confirmed/pending/unknown evidence.
- Pre-dispatch cooldown/GET waits honor Stop and context changes. Forward procurement Stop into its nested contribution transaction; cancel only before dispatch. An exact waiting-only inner record is positive proof for clearing the outer unsent intent. Once a contribution is dispatched, finish receipt verification, independent readback and checkpoint before stopping, and settle the confirmed outer result exactly once. Unknown results remain recoverable rather than being declared unsent.
- Show Chinese cooldown/backoff labels and remaining-wait countdowns. Preserve current scores in stock and purchase progress; add bounded stage/status/attempt/wait diagnostics without account/item identities or raw responses.
- Keep the fixed 30-minute procurement approval, approved budget, frozen per-card caps, quote expiry, FSU/material protection and .45 retirement/isolation rules unchanged. Gallery, ordinary Puzzle and FSU behavior are unchanged.
